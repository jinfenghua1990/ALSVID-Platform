# ALSVID Platform Portal Architecture

One platform with multiple entrances.

## Admin Portal

admin.alsvid.com

Internal employees manage:

- Product
- Service
- Customer
- Dealer
- Operations

## Dealer Portal

dealer.alsvid.com

Dealers access:

- Product catalog
- Dealer orders
- Technical documents
- Service requests

## Service Portal

service.alsvid.com

Customers access:

- My vehicle
- Warranty
- Repair progress
- Manuals

## Principle

Different portals, same platform, same master data, different permissions.
