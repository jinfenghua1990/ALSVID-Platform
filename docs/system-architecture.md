# ALSVID Platform Architecture

- Product pages
- E-commerce

### 2. B2B Dealer Portal
- Dealer login

### 3. ALSVID Platform
- Product management
- Customer vehicle records
- Service center
- Dealer management

### 4. Commerce Operations
- Orders
- Purchasing
- Inventory
